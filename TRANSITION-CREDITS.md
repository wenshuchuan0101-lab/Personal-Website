# Transition design reference

The portfolio transition is an independent implementation of a multi-cursor page-building effect, inspired by [Swarm Rebuild](https://github.com/DhvanilPatel/swarm-cursors) and the [Swarm site by Joel Califa](https://github.com/califa/swarm-site).

The downloaded extension does not provide a reuse license. None of its source code, cursor assets, DOM snapshotting engine, browser-extension logic or audio system is included in this website.

`swarm-transition.js` constructs the actual live DOM and SVG elements through assigned jobs. The profile sequence types each text field character by character; traces rings, 72 individual dial ticks, orbits, the star and button edges; and demonstrates the real two-way name page turn. It schedules over 140 jobs on desktop, with fewer jobs where mobile layouts hide decorative elements. The quick-browse sequence builds the real growth chart, then switches to the real first Images & Film work and constructs its poster, title, credits, award and entry link in the same continuous run. There is one opening and one ending; no page screenshots or remote animation engine are used.

The opening follows three asymmetric curved school paths with staggered follower delays. Every cursor has persistent position and velocity, capped acceleration, smooth heading changes, nearby separation and same-school velocity alignment. Depth controls draw order, size and opacity. The ending steers the distributed cursors into delayed curved queues and out of the viewport; it does not snap them onto a circular formation. Layout jobs still drag an expanding four-sided box before filling the region. During the build, cursors have explicit jobs and inactive cursors stay off screen. The overlays contain arrow cursors and the requested layout boxes, without task labels, status text, crosshairs, I-beams, click rings or glyph boxes.

`settings.js` → `transition` controls `profileMilliseconds` (9.4 seconds), `growthMilliseconds` (9 seconds), colors and cursor limits (32 desktop, 22 mobile). The opening receives about 1.6–1.7 seconds and the ending about 2.1–2.2 seconds. Both are included in the duration. A 10-second watchdog includes initialization; quick browse subtracts its preceding button feedback from that budget. It always restores the page, even if fonts are slow. Escape, viewport changes and hiding the tab also finish the animation; reduced-motion preferences skip it. Skipping quick browse still lands on the final Images & Film work. `PortfolioTransition.inspect()` provides task counts, phases, cursor motion and checked milestones for local verification. No extension installation is required.

The opening and finale were visually reviewed against the two user-supplied notes, with the second as the motion reference: https://xhslink.cn/o/3dqrgIQQrra and https://xhslink.cn/o/7viCLcS9qjt. Reference videos and extracted analysis frames are not included in the published website.

The latest supplied link, https://xhslink.cn/o/8eQjMlfnD1I, resolves to the same second note (`6aed746b000000002203b633`) and original video as the earlier reference. Its opening and final sequences were inspected frame by frame for the timing, loose follower spacing and exit rhythm.
